# Persona Studio

### AI-Powered User Research and Persona Generation

Persona Studio is an AI-powered product research workspace designed to help product managers, UX designers, and developers transform product ideas into structured user personas and actionable research insights. It brings persona generation, pain point analysis, Jobs-to-be-Done, user journey mapping, and persona comparison into a single workspace.

## Live Link

**Explore Persona Studio:** [https://personastudioai.netlify.app/](https://personastudioai.netlify.app/)

## About the Project

Persona Studio streamlines the early stages of product discovery by helping teams organize product context, explore user needs, and generate structured persona hypotheses. It combines AI-assisted research with an intuitive interface to support user-centered product decisions.

The platform is designed to help teams move from initial product assumptions toward clearer research questions and potential product opportunities. It emphasizes research transparency by distinguishing evidence from AI-generated inferences and assumptions.

## Problem It Solves

Understanding users is essential to building successful products, but creating meaningful user personas and translating research into actionable product decisions can be time-consuming and fragmented.

Persona Studio addresses this challenge by bringing persona generation, user pain point analysis, Jobs-to-be-Done, and journey mapping into one workspace. It helps teams organize potential user needs, identify areas for further investigation, and explore product opportunities more efficiently.

The platform also encourages responsible use of AI by treating generated insights as hypotheses rather than verified user feedback.

## Features

- **AI-Powered Persona Generation:** Generate structured user personas from product ideas, target audiences, and product context.
- **Pain Point Analysis:** Explore potential user frustrations, unmet needs, and areas requiring further research.
- **Jobs-to-be-Done (JTBD):** Identify functional, emotional, and social jobs that influence user behavior.
- **User Journey Mapping:** Explore user experiences across different stages and identify potential friction points.
- **Persona Comparison:** Compare multiple personas to understand differences in goals, behaviors, needs, and challenges.
- **AI Research Chat:** Explore research questions and product context through AI-assisted conversations.
- **Persona Editing and Regeneration:** Refine personas and iterate on generated research outputs.
- **Project Management:** Organize research projects and manage persona-related information in a dedicated workspace.
- **Research Transparency:** Distinguish evidence, AI-generated inferences, and hypotheses to support more informed product decisions.
- **Responsive User Interface:** Access a research-focused interface designed for usability across different screen sizes.

## Technologies Used

- **Next.js** - Application framework and routing.
- **React** - Component-based user interface development.
- **TypeScript** - Type safety and maintainable application logic.
- **Tailwind CSS** - Utility-first styling and responsive layouts.
- **shadcn/ui** - Reusable and accessible interface components.
- **Lucide React** - Icons and interface visuals.
- **Groq API** - AI-powered persona generation and research assistance.
- **Zod** - Schema validation and structured data handling.
- **SQLite** - Database persistence for project data.
