## Step 3 — What do you already know?

### Interviews

Remote workers say they struggle to decide which task deserves their attention when meetings and messages interrupt their plans. Several users currently use a calendar, task manager, and timer separately.

### Surveys

Survey respondents frequently mention difficulty maintaining focus during the workday. Many say they want a simpler way to identify their most important task and protect time for it.

### Analytics

Early product analytics show that users create tasks but often do not complete their planned focus sessions. Usage drops when users receive frequent interruptions.

### Reviews

Users of competing productivity tools appreciate task planning and focus timers but often complain that they need multiple tools to manage their complete workflow.

### Competitor observations

Existing tools often specialize in either task management, calendars, time tracking, or focus timers. There is an opportunity to provide a lightweight workflow that connects these activities without becoming a full project-management suite.

### Known pain points

Users lose focus when priorities change, notifications interrupt deep work, and their planning tools are disconnected from their actual calendar and working habits.
